Methods, definitions & publishing guide

Comparisons and uncertainty

Rates are used exactly as supplied. Verify units, age restrictions, age standardization, source, and release status before publication. They are not inferred from counts or population. The comparator is an unweighted state mean, not a pooled US rate.

Trend comparisons use a fixed cohort of other states with complete values in every observed year for the selected sex and measure. The 95% percentile interval resamples complete state trajectories 10,000 times using a seeded generator. It describes between-state variability under an exchangeable-state interpretation, not uncertainty in a literal finite-state mean, and does not propagate within-state rate uncertainty. Iowa trend CIs are intentionally omitted.

Ranks and the ratio

Ranks use the selected year, with 1 = highest and minimum ranks for exact ties. Missing observations are excluded and never replaced with older years. Rank uncertainty is not modeled. Ranking error bars use supplied rate intervals. The ratio is heart death counts / cancer death counts; it is not a ratio of age-adjusted rates. Zero or missing cancer counts yield missing ratios. Ratio intervals are not estimated.

Annual percent change

APC = 100 × [exp(β) − 1], using equal-weight OLS of log(rate) on year. Models require positive, complete rates in every calendar year of the sex-specific common period and at least five years. Primary 95% intervals use residual t inference, with n − 2 degrees of freedom. They assume a log-linear trend with independent, homoscedastic errors; short series and pandemic departures can undermine inference. Intervals are pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted. Both mortality causes appear together; ratios are not used in the APC view.

Deploy to GitHub Pages

Upload this file as index.html at your repository root. Add your original tab-delimited file as data.tsv in the same folder, then enable Pages for that branch in repository Settings. No build, API key, external script, or analytics service is required. Files committed to a public repository are public. Visitors can instead load data locally; locally selected files are not uploaded.

Schema: State, State Code, Sex, Year, Population, Cancer-Deaths, Cancer-Rate, Cancer-Lower, Cancer-Upper, Deaths, Heart-Rate, Heart-Lower, Heart-Upper. “Deaths” must mean heart deaths. Only the 50 US states and Female/Male records are analyzed; excluded records are reported.
